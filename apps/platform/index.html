Authority before convenience.

The platform will not silently infer provider access, mutate repositories, run lifecycle scripts, or grant shell worlds. Transformations occur in derived worlds, produce inspectable semantic diffs and evidence, and default to reviewable pull or merge requests. Missing authority is a refusal—not a hidden fallback.
